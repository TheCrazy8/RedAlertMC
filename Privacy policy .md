RedAlertMC Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

RedAlertMC (“the Bot”) is a Discord-to-Minecraft chat integration service that enables real-time communication between connected Discord channels and Minecraft servers.

This Privacy Policy explains what information RedAlertMC processes, how it is used, and how users can request deletion of any stored information.

2. Information We Process

RedAlertMC may process the following information to provide its services:

Discord Information

* Discord usernames and display names.
* Discord user, channel, and server IDs.
* Messages sent in channels configured for Minecraft integration.

Minecraft Information

* Minecraft usernames.
* Minecraft chat messages.
* Player join and leave events.
* Minecraft server connection information.

Configuration Information

* Connected Discord server and channel IDs.
* Minecraft integration settings.
* Information necessary to establish and maintain connections.

RedAlertMC does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or unrelated private messages.

3. Real-Time Message Processing

RedAlertMC forwards messages between Discord and Minecraft in real time.

* Messages are processed for delivery to the connected platform.
* RedAlertMC does not maintain a permanent database of chat messages.
* Message content is not intentionally retained after successful processing and transmission.
* Discord messages may remain in Discord’s message history.
* Minecraft servers may independently record chat messages in their own logs.

RedAlertMC cannot control or delete information independently retained by Discord, Minecraft server operators, or other users.

4. How Information Is Used

RedAlertMC processes information exclusively for purposes related to its operation, including:

* Relaying messages between Discord and Minecraft.
* Identifying message senders.
* Displaying enabled Minecraft player events.
* Maintaining server connections and configurations.
* Diagnosing technical errors.
* Protecting the Bot against misuse.

RedAlertMC does not sell personal information or use chat content for advertising, profiling, or AI training.

5. Information Sharing

Messages and associated usernames are shared between the Discord channels and Minecraft servers connected through RedAlertMC.

This sharing is necessary for the Bot’s primary functionality.

Messages may become visible to users on the receiving platform who are not members of the originating Discord or Minecraft community.

RedAlertMC does not sell user data to third parties.

6. Data Storage and Retention

RedAlertMC does not permanently store relayed chat messages.

Messages may exist temporarily in memory while being transmitted.

Necessary configuration information may be retained while an integration remains active.

When an integration is disconnected, its configuration information may be deleted when no longer required.

RedAlertMC does not control the retention policies of Discord or independently operated Minecraft servers.

7. Data Security

Reasonable security precautions are used to protect Bot operations and configuration information.

Authentication credentials and connection information should be accessible only to authorized systems and administrators.

Although reasonable safeguards are used, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may request information about personal data held by RedAlertMC and request deletion of eligible stored information.

Since RedAlertMC does not permanently store chat messages, it generally cannot retrieve or delete historical messages from its own storage.

Requests may be submitted through:

Contact: [link](https://discord.gg/RedAlertWX)

Requests will be handled according to applicable laws and platform requirements.

9. Server Administrator Responsibilities

Server administrators are responsible for notifying members that cross-platform chat integration is enabled.

Administrators must ensure that designated channels and Minecraft servers are configured appropriately.

They are also responsible for their own server logs, moderation policies, and independently stored information.

10. Third-Party Services

RedAlertMC interacts with Discord and Minecraft, which have their own terms and privacy policies.

RedAlertMC is independently developed and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Discord, Mojang Studios, or Microsoft.

11. Children’s Privacy

RedAlertMC must be used in accordance with Discord’s minimum-age requirements and applicable privacy laws.

The Bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as RedAlertMC evolves.

Material changes will be announced through the Bot’s official support channels where appropriate.
